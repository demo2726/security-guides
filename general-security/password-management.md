**Password Management**

**Importance of Strong Passwords**

First line of defense against unauthorized access.

Weak/reused passwords are a leading cause of breaches.

**Password Complexity**

Minimum length (e.g., 12+ characters).

Use mix of upper/lowercase, numbers, and special characters.

Avoid dictionary words, names, or predictable patterns.

**Password Hygiene**

Do not reuse passwords across accounts.

Change default system/application passwords immediately.

Rotate passwords periodically (based on policy or compromise risk).

**Storage & Management**

Use a password manager (KeePass, Bitwarden, 1Password, etc.).

Never write passwords in plain text or share them via email/chat.

Secure password databases with strong master passphrase + MFA.

**Authentication Enhancements**

Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) wherever possible.

Prefer passphrases over complex but short strings.

Consider passwordless solutions (FIDO2, biometrics, hardware keys).

**Organizational Policies**

Enforce password requirements via system settings (PAM, AD policies).

Monitor for credential leaks (dark web monitoring, haveibeenpwned).

Educate users about phishing/social engineering risks.

**Best Practices**

Lock accounts after repeated failed login attempts.

Avoid forced frequent password changes (leads to weaker choices).

Audit and remove stale/unused accounts.
