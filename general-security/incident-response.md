# Incident Response

Incident Response (IR) is the structured process of identifying, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity incidents. A strong IR plan minimizes damage, reduces recovery time, and helps prevent future incidents.

---

## Why Incident Response Matters

- Cyberattacks are inevitable — effective response reduces the impact.  
- A well-documented IR process ensures consistent handling of incidents.  
- Required by many compliance standards (HIPAA, PCI DSS, ISO 27001, NIST).  

---

## The Incident Response Lifecycle

According to **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 2** and **SANS Institute**, incident response typically follows these six phases:

1. **Preparation**  
   - Develop and train an incident response team (IRT).  
   - Define policies, playbooks, communication channels, and escalation paths.  
   - Ensure monitoring, logging, and backups are in place.  

2. **Identification**  
   - Detect and confirm potential incidents (alerts, anomalies, threat intelligence).  
   - Classify severity and scope of the incident.  

3. **Containment**  
   - Short-term: isolate affected systems to stop the spread.  
   - Long-term: apply temporary fixes, block malicious IPs/domains, strengthen defenses.  

4. **Eradication**  
   - Remove malware, close exploited vulnerabilities, disable compromised accounts.  
   - Patch and harden systems to prevent recurrence.  

5. **Recovery**  
   - Safely restore systems and services.  
   - Monitor for signs of re-infection or further compromise.  

6. **Lessons Learned**  
   - Conduct a post-incident review.  
   - Update IR playbooks, policies, and training based on findings.  
   - Share knowledge with stakeholders (and ISACs if applicable).  

---

## Key Roles in Incident Response

- **Incident Response Team Lead** – coordinates response efforts.  
- **Forensic Analyst** – investigates artifacts and determines root cause.  
- **Communications Officer** – manages internal and external communications.  
- **Legal & Compliance** – ensures reporting obligations are met.  
- **Management/Executives** – make business continuity and escalation decisions.  

---

## Best Practices

- Maintain updated **IR playbooks** for common scenarios (phishing, ransomware, insider threats).  
- Conduct regular **tabletop exercises** and simulations.  
- Centralize **logging and monitoring** (SIEM, EDR).  
- Establish **clear communication plans** (who to contact, when, and how).  
- Define thresholds for involving **law enforcement** or regulators.  
- Keep backups **offline and tested**.
